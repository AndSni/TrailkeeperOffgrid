Trailkeeper Offgrid is a field app for trail maintenance crews and volunteers who work where there's no signal. Everything runs on the phone: no account, no backend, no sync, and no data leaves the device except an optional map-tile download or something you explicitly choose to share.

Track your work:
* Projects group trails, tasks, structures, and work logs for an area.
* Tasks carry a priority, a location, photos, and a running note thread - log what's broken and what you did about it.
* Record a route while you ride or walk it, with live distance and bearing back to the start.
* Log structures (bridges, culverts, boardwalks) and their inspections over time.
* Time segment work sessions against a trail.
* File a trail condition report from the map, with photos and severity.

Built for no signal:
* Pre-download map regions to the phone before heading out; the map, trail overlays, and GPS puck all work with zero connectivity once downloaded.
* Every GPS fix comes straight from the phone's GNSS chip (GPS_PROVIDER) - no network-based positioning, no Google Play Services.
* Local reminders nudge you about overdue inspections and tasks - no server round-trip required.

Your data stays yours:
* Share a single project, task, route, trail, or structure straight to a teammate's phone through the normal share sheet (Bluetooth, Quick Share, email, whatever you've already got) - they get a preview before anything is imported, never a silent merge.
* A full-workspace backup is a single .zip file you control - back it up, move it to a new phone, or hand it to a teammate.
* Import and export routes and trails as GPX, so this plays well with other GPS tools.
* Multi-format coordinates (decimal, DMS, UTM) with one-tap copy, for radioing a position or dropping it into another app.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.
